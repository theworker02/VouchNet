# VouchNet brand

VouchNet is a professional network built around credible work, deliberate relationships, and
human-controlled automation. The brand should communicate calm confidence and evidence—not
status chasing or engagement farming.

## Official assets

- Vector mark: `apps/web/public/brand/vouchnet-mark.svg`
- Raster mark: `apps/web/public/brand/vouchnet-mark.png`

The mark can be used to accurately link to VouchNet, describe a verified VouchNet integration, or
refer to the product editorially. Preserve its aspect ratio, use the original file, and leave clear
space around it.

Do not recolor, distort, animate deceptively, combine with another mark, or imply that VouchNet
endorses a product, profile, automation, or employment claim. Never use the mark in a way that
suggests machine activity is human activity.

## Voice

Use direct, evidence-led language: “vouch,” “proof of work,” “professional context,” and “human
approval.” Avoid terms that encourage follower counts, artificial reach, or anonymous ratings.
